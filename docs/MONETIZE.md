# Monetization Strategy

**Canadian Retirement Calculator - Revenue & Retention Playbook**

---

## The Core Problem

Retirement calculators are **"calculate once, decide, leave forever"** tools. That's why subscription models fail for most users. Unlike budgeting apps (YNAB, Monarch) where you engage daily, retirement planning is episodic.

**The question isn't "how do I get $15/month forever" - it's "what do people actually pay for around retirement planning?"**

---

## Competitive Landscape

| Product | Model | Price | Why It Works |
|---------|-------|-------|--------------|
| **ProjectionLab** | Annual + Lifetime | $109/yr or $1,199 once | Lifetime creates urgency, eliminates churn |
| **Boldin** | Annual + Coaching upsell | $144/yr + $150/session | Tool is gateway to human help |
| **Fidelity** | Free tool → AUM fees | $0 → manage your money | Loss leader for bigger sale |

**The pattern:** The calculator is the hook, not the product.

## 10 Creative Monetization Ideas

### TIER 1: Quick Wins (Build This Month)

#### 1. The "Lifetime Deal" Option

- **Price:** $199-299 one-time
- **Why it works:** ProjectionLab sells this for $1,199. Canadians are underserved. A $249 lifetime deal for "the only accurate Canadian retirement calculator" is compelling.
- **Retention built-in:** They've paid, so they WANT to use it. Updates them annually.
- **Launch strategy:** AppSumo-style launch, Product Hunt, Reddit r/PersonalFinanceCanada

---

#### 2. Beautiful PDF Report (One-Time Purchase)

- **Price:** $19-39 per report
- **What:** A gorgeous, personalized 15-20 page PDF they can print, share with spouse, or bring to an advisor.

**Contents:**
- Year-by-year projections table
- Income sources breakdown (CPP, OAS, pension, withdrawals)
- Tax efficiency score with explanation
- "5 ways to improve your plan" section
- Beautiful charts and visualizations
- Provincial tax summary

**Why it works:** People want something TANGIBLE. A PDF feels real. They can hand it to their spouse and say "look, we're okay."

**Repeat revenue:** "Get your 2026 Retirement Report" - annual touchpoint.

---

#### 3. AI "Second Opinion" Analysis

- **Price:** $9-19 per analysis
- **What:** After they run their scenario, offer: *"Want AI to find ways to optimize your plan?"*

**Output:** 5-7 personalized insights:
- "Delay CPP to 70 could add $47K lifetime income"
- "Your RRSP withdrawal rate is creating OAS clawback in years 71-74"
- "Consider a $15K Roth conversion in years 65-67 while income is low"
- "Your TFSA is underutilized - prioritize contributions here"

**Why it works:** They already trust the calculator. This is the "what should I DO about it?" answer.

---

### TIER 2: Moderate Effort, Strong Upside

#### 4. Annual "What Changed For YOU" Email

- **Price:** Free (retention mechanism) → drives paid features
- **What:** Every January, email users with personalized updates

**Email content:**
> *"Your 2026 retirement projection is $23K better than 2025. Here's why..."*
>
> - TFSA limit increased to $7,500 (+$500)
> - CPP maximum pensionable earnings increased 4.2%
> - OAS clawback threshold now $90,997 (was $86,912)
> - New federal tax bracket indexed for inflation
>
> **[View Your Updated Plan →]**

**Why it works:** Creates an annual habit. They think of you every January. Natural upsell moment.

**The Canadian Angle:** Tax rules change annually. This is valuable and unique. US tools can't do this.

---

#### 5. Monte Carlo Stress Test (Paywalled Feature)

- **Price:** Included in $49-99/year "Pro" tier OR $29 one-time unlock
- **What:** "What are the chances your money lasts?" Run 1,000 simulations with varying market returns.

**Output:**
- Success probability percentage
- Confidence bands on portfolio chart
- "Worst case" and "best case" scenarios
- Recommendations to improve odds

**Why it works:** This is what ProjectionLab and Boldin paywall. It's the #1 anxiety reducer for retirees.

**Simple upgrade prompt:**
> *"Your plan works in our base case. But what if markets crash early in retirement? [Run Stress Test - $29]"*

---

#### 6. Advisor Referral Revenue (Affiliate)

- **Price:** $0 to user, $50-200 per qualified lead to you
- **What:** After calculation, offer connection to vetted professionals

**Implementation:**
> *"Want a professional to review your plan? Here are fee-only advisors in Ontario who specialize in retirement income planning."*
>
> - [Advisor Name] - CFP, Fee-only, Specializes in corporate pensions
> - [Advisor Name] - CFP, Hourly rates, Focus on tax optimization

**Partner with:** Fee-only planners (not AUM advisors) - aligns with user trust

**Revenue potential:** 5 referrals/month × $100 = $6K/year passive

**Why it works:** You're not competing with advisors - you're feeding them qualified leads. They'll pay for that.

### TIER 3: Bigger Bets, Bigger Payoff

#### 7. "Retirement Coaching" Marketplace

- **Price:** $99-199 per 1-hour session (you take 25-30%)
- **What:** Curate a network of 5-10 Canadian CFPs/retirement specialists. Users book through your platform.

**How it works:**
1. Vet and onboard 5-10 advisors across provinces
2. Advisors set their own rates ($99-199/hour)
3. Users book directly through your platform
4. You take 25-30% platform fee

**Your cut:** $25-50 per session × 20 sessions/month = $500-1,000/month

**Trust factor:** You've already built trust with the calculator. You're the natural bridge to human help.

---

#### 8. Scenario Packs / Templates

- **Price:** $9-19 each

**Examples:**
| Pack | Description | Target User |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| **Early Retirement (FIRE)** | Pre-built scenarios for retiring at 45, 50, 55 | FIRE community |
| **Pension Optimizer** | Bridge benefits, indexed vs non-indexed, early vs normal | DB pension holders |
| **Snowbird Pack** | Canadian/US tax implications for winter travelers | Seasonal migrants |
| **Widow/Widower** | What happens when spouse passes, survivor benefits | Estate planning |
| **Encore Career** | Part-time work in retirement, phased retirement | Semi-retirees |

**Why it works:** People don't know what scenarios to run. You've already built the what-if logic - package it.

---

#### 9. Canadian Tax Calculation API

- **Price:** $0.05-0.10 per calculation OR $99-299/month subscription
- **What:** Let OTHER Canadian fintech apps use your tax engine

**What you're selling:**
- Federal + provincial tax calculations for all 13 jurisdictions
- CPP/OAS benefit calculations with age adjustments
- RRIF minimum withdrawal calculations
- OAS clawback calculations
- Marginal and effective tax rate calculations

**Potential customers:**
- Canadian robo-advisors
- Budgeting apps adding retirement features
- Credit unions building member tools
- HR platforms with retirement benefits

**Why it works:** You've built something hard. Others need this. This is infrastructure that compounds.

#### 10. Physical "Retirement Workbook" + Tool Bundle

- **Price:** $39-79 (print-on-demand via Lulu or Amazon KDP)

**Workbook contents:**
- Values clarification exercises

**Bundle option:** "Workbook + Lifetime Calculator Access" for $149

**Why it works:** Physical products feel premium. Workbook owners are more engaged. Great gift item for adult children to give parents.

## The Retention Hack: Reframe as "Dashboard"

The real retention play isn't selling more features. It's **changing the mental model**.

| Old Framing | New Framing |
|-------------|-------------|
| "Calculate your retirement once" | "Your Retirement Dashboard - updated with every tax change, market shift, and life event" |

### Practical Implementation

1. **Email them when something changes**
   - "2026 TFSA limit increased to $7,500. Your plan updated."
   - "CPP rates changed. See how it affects your projection."

2. **Track progress over time**
   - Manual net worth entry (quarterly prompt)
   - Show a chart of their trajectory over time
   - Compare "projected vs actual" as years pass

3. **Celebrate milestones**
   - "You hit $500K saved! You're 67% to your goal."
   - "Congratulations! At current pace, you can retire 2 years early."

4. **Annual checkup prompt**
   - "It's been 6 months since you updated your plan. Anything change?"
   - "Your birthday is coming up - time for your annual retirement checkup?"

This turns episodic use into habitual use.

## The Bottom Line

**Don't sell subscriptions to a calculator. Sell:**

1. **Peace of mind** - PDF they can hold, stress test results
2. **Personalized answers** - AI analysis, scenario packs
3. **Human connection** - Advisor referrals, coaching marketplace
4. **The annual habit** - Tax law updates that affect THEIR specific plan

The calculator is the trust-builder. The upsells are where you make money.
